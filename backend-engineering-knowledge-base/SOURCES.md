## Source-use policy

- A source appearing in a paper means it is relevant to one or more engineering claims or trade-offs; it does not imply every paragraph is normative text from that source.
- RFCs and formal specifications define protocol behavior. Operational guidance may impose stronger application-level requirements.
- Provider documentation is scoped to that provider and version. Do not generalize it to other providers.
- Books and engineering articles provide models and failure experience; verify concrete platform behavior against official documentation.
- Recheck living documents and current product versions before implementation.
