## The important structural change

Your knowledge base should probably have **three layers**:

```text
backend-knowledge/
├── primitives/
│   ├── time/
│   ├── identifiers/
│   ├── decimal/
│   ├── validation/
│   ├── errors/
│   └── state-machines/
│
├── systems/
│   ├── authentication/
│   ├── authorization/
│   ├── database/
│   ├── caching/
│   ├── queues/
│   ├── webhooks/
│   ├── files/
│   ├── search/
│   └── realtime/
│
└── cross-cutting/
    ├── security/
    ├── concurrency/
    ├── idempotency/
    ├── reliability/
    ├── observability/
    ├── performance/
    ├── scalability/
    ├── migrations/
    └── testing/
```

The key idea is that **“authentication” is a system**, while things such as **idempotency, concurrency, time, identifiers, consistency, retries, and validation are reusable reasoning primitives** that may affect dozens of systems.

That distinction will make this database much stronger for coding agents than simply organizing everything as feature names.
